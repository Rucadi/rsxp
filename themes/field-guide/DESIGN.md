# Field Guide

Field Guide is the Pokémon-focused RSXP theme. It should feel like opening a
well-used regional field notebook or Pokédex: route-map cream (`#F2EBDD`),
clean card paper (`#FFFDF5`), Pokédex red (`#D83A3A`), trainer blue (`#2D6FA3`),
and badge gold (`#D9A62E`).

Use red for the main Play action and important dex selections, blue for links,
search, and information, and gold for badges, forms, and featured discoveries.
Keep status labels explicit so color is never the only way to identify a
shiny, custom form, encounter, or compatibility state.

Species, item, and move views may use small type or habitat accents, but the
global shell stays neutral enough for original Pokémon fangames and other RPG
Maker projects. Prefer original field-guide motifs, route markers, silhouettes,
and game-provided artwork. Do not reproduce official logos, Pokédex screens,
type charts, character art, or proprietary windowskins in the launcher.

Cards should lead with the game's artwork and title. A dex-like detail panel
can show a sprite, number, name, forms, locations, and notes with clear tabs.
Keep the player viewport untouched and let the game provide its own battle and
menu presentation.
