# Moonlit Grove

Moonlit Grove is the quiet night theme for long RPG sessions. Use deep green
surfaces (`#172722`, `#22382F`) with cream text (`#F4F0E3`), mint actions
(`#8BCDAB`), sky-blue links (`#95C9ED`), and gold chapter accents
(`#E7BE65`).

The player chrome should recede around gameplay. Prefer flat panels, soft 2px
shadows, and clear focus rings. Reserve gold for highlights and warnings, and
use coral (`#F39A9A`) for errors. Do not tint or filter the game's framebuffer.
